05 — Design details

The card as supplied. The site in ISU identity.

The card is a CR80 portrait (53.98 × 85.60 mm) laid out on an 816 × 1296 px grid. The lanyard is a 900 × 20 mm printed strip. The card and lanyard artwork is untouched; this page’s own colours and type follow the ISU Corporate Visual Identity Manual (2026 edition).

Branding

  • The seal appears only in its supplied full-colour form: never stretched, cropped, recoloured, outlined, glowed, shadowed, used as a watermark or placed over a photograph.
  • The Torch, Circle Rays, Gear and Weave Rope are never separated or removed. Nothing on this page is highlighted by lighting the seal.
  • Core values on the strap — People, Nature, Entrepreneurship, Innovation — carry the manual’s colour meanings in the tour: Yellow for People, Green for Nature, Red for Entrepreneurship, Blue for Innovation.

Verify before official use. The seal in the supplied files reads “1978”. The 2026 manual introduces a new official seal (BOR Resolution No. 114 s. 2025). Swap in the master files from the University Strategic Communications Office. Hardware and the badge holder are an illustrative model, not an ISU specification.

Template note: the supplied front reads “Cabagan, Isabela” while the back certifies “Echague Campus”. Both are shown exactly as supplied. Photo, name, student number, college and contact lines are placeholders.
